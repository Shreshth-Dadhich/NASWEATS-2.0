EarthSystem / Editorial Research UI System


1. Aesthetic Identity & Design Philosophy




Atmosphere: Deep navy near-black field, editorial warmth from desert sand/gold accents, crisp cyan/teal telemetry, and subtle technical gridlines.


Tone: High-credibility scientific research and investigative journalism (reminiscent of NASA mission dashboards combined with high-end editorial science publications).


Lighting: Layered radial atmosphere gradients over deep navy (--gradient-atmosphere), dark glassmorphic sticky navigation, and inset border highlights on cards.


Data Integrity: Tabular monospaced numerals, strict significance badging (FDR p-value status), and linear chart interpolation (no artificial smoothing).

Header: Fixed height h-16, sticky top-0 z-40, background bg-background/75 backdrop-blur-xl, border border-b border-border/60.

B. Segmented Button Control (Segmented.tsx)




Used for switching variable timescales (Annual vs Monthly vs Trends) and regional filters.


Exterior container: inline-flex gap-1 rounded-lg border border-border bg-background/60 p-1.


Active button: bg-primary text-primary-foreground font-medium rounded-md px-3 py-1.5 text-sm.


Inactive button: text-muted-foreground hover:bg-surface-raised hover:text-foreground.




C. Statistical Significance Badges (SignificanceBadge.tsx)




Significant (FDR p < 0.05): border-sand/40 bg-sand/10 text-sand with ArrowUpRight or ArrowDownRight.


Not Significant: border-muted bg-muted/30 text-muted-foreground with Minus icon.


Directional Coloring: Coral/amber for positive change (--positive), deep cool teal for negative change (--negative).






7. Data Visualization & Map Conventions




No Curved Overshoots: All line charts use linear interpolation (type="linear" in Recharts/SVG) to avoid fabricating intermediate points.


Chart Tooltip: Styled as .panel-inset with mono coordinate time tags, colored series squares, and exact decimal values.


Map Theming: Dark vector base tile layer (e.g., Esri World Dark Gray Base) with custom continuous color ramps:



Heat/Temperature: Violet 
→
→ Amber 
→
→ Bright Gold


Moisture/Rain: Sand 
→
→ Cyan 
→
→ Deep Indigo


Dust/Aerosol: Pale Sand 
→
→ Terracotta 
→
→ Deep Rust
